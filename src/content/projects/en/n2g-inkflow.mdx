# N2G Inkflow — Studio Management Platform & Media Vault

Advanced management platform engineered to streamline creative studio operations: from client onboarding and project intake requests to interactive calendar scheduling and authenticated media asset storage.

## Architectural Highlights

- **FullCalendar Engine**: Visual scheduling across month/week/day and agenda appointment views, with 15-minute slot intervals, Europe/Rome timezone resolution, and UTC transport.
- **AG Grid Client Directory**: High-performance paginated data grid with server-side query filtering and instant updates.
- **End-to-End Request Pipeline**: Integrated workflow connecting clients, design requests, and scheduled bookings into single atomic transactions.
- **Security & RBAC**: In-memory Bearer tokens paired with HttpOnly refresh cookies and strict role-based access control.
