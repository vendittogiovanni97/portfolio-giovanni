## Two Industries, One Pattern

Tattoo studios and insurance agencies have nothing in common — except they both run on WhatsApp, Excel, and paper. My job was to replace that stack with software that actually fits their workflow.

## Lesson 1: Workflows > Features

Don't build features. Build workflows. A tattoo studio doesn't need a "booking system." They need a flow: client inquiries → deposit → design approval → appointment → aftercare. Map the entire journey.

## Lesson 2: Domain Expertise is Non-Negotiable

I spent two weeks in tattoo shops before writing a line of code. Same at Agilae — I sat with insurance agents, watched them work, understood their pain points. The best SaaS products come from deep domain understanding.

## Lesson 3: Offline-First Matters

Tattoo artists work in basements with bad WiFi. Insurance agents work from home, in cafés, on trains. Build for intermittent connectivity. Sync when you can, work always.

## Lesson 4: Simple UI Wins

Complex UIs fail in vertical SaaS. The user isn't a developer — they're a tattoo artist who wants to check tomorrow's appointments. Every extra click is a reason to go back to WhatsApp.

## The Pattern

Whether it's tattoos or insurance:
1. Map the real workflow
2. Build for the environment
3. Keep it simple
4. Make it fast

The technology is the easy part. Understanding the user is the hard part.
